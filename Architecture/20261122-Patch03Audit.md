# Audit of Patch 03 (`20261122-Patch03.md`)

1. §6.2 | Contradiction | §6.2 claims that parenting the role box to the skills panel makes it inherit "the panel's strata and opacity" (`Features/EquippedSkills.lua:519-530`). In `Core/PanelChrome.lua:162-166` and `:204-208`, `PanelChrome.Build` fixes `panel:SetAlpha(1)` on the frame and applies `spec.alpha` only to `chrome.background:SetAlpha`, and `PanelChrome.SetAlpha` updates only `chrome.background:SetAlpha`. Parenting the box to `state.panel` therefore does not inherit `panelAlpha` or reflect changes from the "Panel opacity" slider. | Track the box's `chrome` table in `Features/EquippedSkills.lua` and call `PanelChrome.SetAlpha(boxChrome, state.settings.panelAlpha)` in `render`, `showPlaceholders`, and `onConfigChanged("panelAlpha")`.

2. §14.1, §6.2 | Contradiction | §14.1 states `"Cross-phase amendments. None is expected"`, while §6.2 creates the role box at `enable()` beside the panel (`EquippedSkills.lua:515-540`, after `rowPool` is prewarmed), parented to `state.panel` (`_G.PersonalAddonEquippedSkills`) and anchored to `state.panel`. In `Tools/harness/tests8.lua:45-70`, `shownChildren(panel)` collects every shown child of `_G.PersonalAddonEquippedSkills` without filtering for row fields, and `rowsText()` selects the last shown child whose `points[1][2] == panel` and calls `nextRow.level:GetText()`. With the role box shown as a child of `panel`, `tests8.lua:215` will select the role box and raise on `nextRow.level` (`nil`). | Update `tests8.lua`'s `shownChildren` or `rowsText` to filter for skill-row frames (as `Tools/harness/tests20.lua:290` does with `rawget(frame, "share")`), and record the amendment in §14.1.

3. §9, §5.7.2 | Ambiguity | §9 states that under `Auto`, `/pa role` prints `Auto, now Tank | Auto, now DPS | Auto, unread` and `the game's tank test: true | false | unread; reads plain=<n> unread=<n>`. However, §5.7.2 and §9 keep separate `autoRoleReadsPlain` and `autoRoleReadsUnread` counters on each of `nameplates`, `threatPanel`, and `equippedSkills`, and §5.7.2 lists only the plates sweep, threat panel sweep, and box relight as callers of `resolve_role`. §9 leaves unspecified which feature's counters `reads plain=<n> unread=<n>` displays (or whether it sums them), and whether `/pa role` reads `resolve_role` live or reads a cached `auto` field from `Nameplates` (which would be `NotAsked` or stale if `nameplates` is disabled or no sweep ran after `/pa role auto`, e.g., check A10). | Specify in §9 whether `/pa role` invokes `resolve_role` live or reads a named feature's cached state, what it reports when `nameplates` and `equippedSkills` are disabled, and which feature's `autoRoleReadsPlain`/`autoRoleReadsUnread` counters it prints.

4. §5.5, §9 | Missing branch | §5.5 updates `ThreatPanel`'s `state.roleChoice` and resolves `state.role` inside `refreshPalette()`, which runs only inside `ThreatPanel`'s `sweep()` (`Features/ThreatPanel.lua:770`), and `ThreatPanel` sweeps only while `state.inCombat` is true (`ThreatPanel.lua:754`). Out of combat—before the first fight of a session, or after changing the role via the box or `/pa role` between fights—`ThreatPanel`'s `roleChoice`, `role`, and `auto` remain uninitialized or stale, causing `ThreatPanel.Inspect()` and `/pa threat` to disagree with `/pa plates` and `/pa role`. | Specify how `ThreatPanel` initializes `roleChoice`, `role`, and `auto` in `enable()` and how `ThreatPanel.Inspect()` / `/pa threat` resolves or reports them out of combat after a role change.

5. §6.4, §6.5, §6.7, §9 | Missing branch | §9 routes `/pa role tank|dps|auto` through `on_role_clicked`'s path and counts `roleWrites`/`roleWritesRefused` under `equippedSkills`. First, `Features/EquippedSkills.lua` currently does not bind `state.counters` to `ns.Diagnostics.CountersFor("equippedSkills")`, and if `equippedSkills` is disabled (§6.7, check B10) or failed `enable()` before `ensurePanel()` (`EquippedSkills.lua:809-814`), `enable()` never runs to bind `state.counters` or create the box. Second, `relight_role_box` has precondition `the box exists` (§6.5), but §6.4 does not guard `relight_role_box` when the box does not exist or `equippedSkills` is disabled. Third, §6.5 omits calling `relight_role_box` when `roleChoice` is toggled on in `onConfigChanged` while bags or preview are open, and during Preview (`showPlaceholders`) where `state.poll` is not running. | Bind `equippedSkills`'s diagnostics table outside `enable()` (or look it up on write), guard `relight_role_box` in `on_role_clicked` on box creation and enabled state, and add `showPlaceholders` and `onConfigChanged("roleChoice")` to `relight_role_box`'s callers.

6. §1.4, §4.3, §9 | Contradiction | §1.4 defines `TickStop = enum { Cast, FightEnded, Backstop, SwitchedOff, LoadingScreen, Disabled }`, and §4.3's `stop_ticks(reason: TickStop)` specifies `counted tickStops<Reason>` (noting only the `Cast` variation `tickStopsCast`). §9 omits `tickStopsDisabled` from both `/pa fsr`'s `stops:` line and the "New counters" table. | Either add `tickStopsDisabled` to §9's `/pa fsr` line and counter table, or state in §4.3 that `stop_ticks(Disabled)` is not counted.

7. §4.2, §4.3, §4.4 | Missing branch | §4.2 specifies that shifting into a non-mana form mid-tick hides the line while keeping `state == Ticking`, and shifting back shows it again (check T13). §4.4 runs `OnUpdate`—the sole caller of `check_fight_at_tick`—only while the line is shown (`FiveSecondRule.lua:168-189`). While shifted out of a mana form, the backstop never runs, so if `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` is missed or refused (§4.5) ticks remain active indefinitely; when shifting back into a mana form out of combat, `refreshVisibility()` (`FiveSecondRule.lua:179-180`) calls `positionMarker()` and `marker:Show()` before `OnUpdate` runs, flashing the gold tick line out of combat for at least one frame. | Keep `OnUpdate` calling `check_fight_at_tick` whenever `state == Ticking` (skipping `positionMarker` while not a mana user), or run `check_fight_at_tick` in `refreshVisibility` / `onDisplayPower` before showing `marker`.

8. §4.3, §9 | Missing branch | §4.3's `tick_fraction(now, clock)` requires `pre: clock.anchor is set`, and `stop_ticks` clears the clock (`clock.anchor` becomes `0` or `nil`). §9 adds `tickFraction` to `FiveSecondRule.Inspect()`, which is callable in `Regenerating` and `InFsr`; calling `tick_fraction` when `clock.anchor` is `0` yields a non-zero `(now mod 2.0) / 2.0` outside `Ticking` (or errors if `nil`). | Specify that `FiveSecondRule.Inspect().tickFraction` returns `0` whenever `state ~= Ticking`.

9. §6.2, §8.1 | Missing branch | §6.2 anchors the box's `TOPLEFT` and `TOPRIGHT` to the skills panel so it tracks `panelWidth` (`74` to `240` px), with `Tank` and `DPS` side by side on the first row and `Auto` full-width on the second. §8.1 leaves "the box's button height" unvalued, and neither §6.2 nor §8.1 specifies the box's height, internal padding/gap, or how `Tank` and `DPS` are resized when `panelWidth` changes (`EquippedSkills.lua:618-629`, `applyWidth`). Because WoW anchors cannot split a variable-width frame into equal halves without explicit widths, `Tank` and `DPS` will stay at their initial widths when the user changes "Window width". | Specify the button height, inner padding/gap, and box height in §6.2/§8.1, and update `applyWidth()` in `Features/EquippedSkills.lua` to recompute and set `Tank` and `DPS` button widths.

10. §6.4, §5.5, §6.1 | Contradiction | §6.4 states `"The click itself makes no client read; relighting under Auto makes resolve_role's one."` When `nameplates` is enabled, `on_role_clicked` synchronously calls `Registry.NotifyConfigChanged("nameplates", "threatRole")`, which runs `Nameplates`' `sweep()` during the click (§5.5, §6.1). Under `Auto`, that sweep calls `resolve_role(Auto)` (`UnitHasEffectivelyTankAura("player")`) plus all nameplate sweep client reads during the click, and `relight_role_box` then calls `resolve_role(Auto)` a second time. | Revise §6.4 to state that `on_role_clicked` makes no client read before `NotifyConfigChanged`, and that `NotifyConfigChanged` runs `Nameplates`' synchronous sweep (including one `resolve_role` read under `Auto`) before `relight_role_box` makes its own `resolve_role` read.

11. §1.4, §3.3, §9 | Undefined reference | §3.3 uses `NameAnchorReading` and `NameColourDecision` in `decide_name_colour`'s signature without defining or referencing them in §1.4 (unlike `PlateRecord` and `NameColourSkip`). Separately, in `Core/Debug.lua:239-248`, `/pa plates` prints the `unreadable:` breakdown line only when `left:`'s `unreadable` sum is above zero; §9 specifies `unreadable: gains faction=<n>` without specifying that `left:`'s `unreadable` sum also includes `FactionUnreadable` (needed for check F10 when only faction is unreadable), and does not state whether `ThreatPanel.COUNTER_NAMES` (`Features/ThreatPanel.lua:58-64`) includes `autoRoleReadsPlain` and `autoRoleReadsUnread` in `/pa threat`. | Define `NameAnchorReading` and `NameColourDecision` in §1.4 as Phase 22's types, state in §9 that `left:`'s `unreadable` sum includes `FactionUnreadable`, and clarify whether `ThreatPanel.COUNTER_NAMES` includes the two Auto role counters.
