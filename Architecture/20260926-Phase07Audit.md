1. 8. Failure modes | Undefined reference | The failure modes table names `levelOf` as the read that fails when a quest's level is unreadable. However, a client removal does not assign a fresh-add type and does not add a watch to a position, making it impossible for a removal to answer S2 or S3. | Clarify that S2 and S3 are answered specifically by the first watch *added*, and that a removal only answers S1.
3. 6.5 Design | Undefined reference | The `rewrite_watch_suffix` failure behaviour states that removed quests that cannot be re-added are "surfaced with their titles". 4. 5.5 Where it plugs in | Missing branch | The seam sequence specifies getting "standing from classify_disposition". The sweep logic misses a branch to define what the standing becomes (presumably `Unreadable`) when `check_disposition_capability` results in `Withheld` and `classify_disposition` cannot be called. | Update the seam logic to explicitly bypass `classify_disposition` and assign an `Unreadable` standing when the capability is `Withheld`.
